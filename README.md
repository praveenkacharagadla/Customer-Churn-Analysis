Customer Churn Analysis — Excel

Project Overview

This project analyzes a Customer Churn dataset using Microsoft Excel to identify the key factors influencing customer attrition and understand why customers discontinue services.

The objective is to demonstrate practical, end-to-end data analysis using Excel, including data cleaning, exploratory analysis, PivotTable-based analysis, data visualization, and business insight generation.

Business Requirements

1. Churn Analysis

- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Identify customer segments with the highest churn rates.
- Compare customers who churned with those who remained.

2. Contract Analysis

- Evaluate churn across different contract types.
- Determine which contract plans have the highest customer turnover.
- Identify contract types associated with better customer retention.

3. Tenure Analysis

- Analyze the relationship between customer tenure and churn.
- Identify tenure periods where customers are most likely to leave.
- Compare churn between new and long-term customers.

4. Monthly Charges Analysis

- Examine how monthly charges influence customer churn.
- Identify pricing segments associated with higher churn rates.
- Compare average monthly charges between churned and retained customers.

5. Payment Method Analysis

- Determine churn patterns across different payment methods.
- Identify payment methods associated with higher churn.
- Compare retention performance across payment methods.

6. Internet Service Analysis

- Compare churn among different internet service types.
- Identify internet services associated with higher customer attrition.
- Analyze the relationship between internet service and customer retention.

7. Demographic Analysis

Evaluate churn across different customer demographics:

- Gender
- Senior Citizen
- Partner Status
- Dependents

Key Insights

The analysis identifies the major factors associated with customer churn, including:

- Customer contract type
- Customer tenure
- Monthly charges
- Payment method
- Internet service type
- Customer demographics
  

Example:

- The overall customer churned from the company are 1869.
- Customers who are took month to month contract are higher in churn count.
- New customers exhibit a higher churn rate than long-term customers, highlighting the importance of early customer engagement and retention.
- Customers in the monthly charge ranges of 70–100 show the highest churn risk, and churn tends to increase as monthly charges rise.
- Customers paying via electronic check have the highest churn rate among all payment methods.
- Fiber optic internet customers demonstrate a higher likelihood of churn, suggesting potential dissatisfaction with the service offering.
- Gender has little impact on churn, single customers are more likely to leave the company than married customers.

---

Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, organizations can consider the following retention strategies:

- The company should focus on targeted retention initiatives and personalized engagement strategies for single customers to reduce churn.
- Attract customers to take year contract by adding attractive subscriptions and additional features to grab them and reduce churn count.
- The company should prioritize onboarding, personalized support, and targeted retention initiatives to encourage long-term customer loyalty.
- The company should strengthen the value proposition of higher-priced plans and implement targeted retention strategies for customers in these pricing segments.
- The company should encourage customers to switch to lower-risk payment methods through incentives and improved payment experiences to enhance customer retention.
- Improving service quality, customer support, and value perception for fiber optic plans can help increase customer retention and reduce churn.
