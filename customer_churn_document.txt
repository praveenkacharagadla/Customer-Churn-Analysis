# Customer Churn Analysis

Objective :

The objective is to demonstrate end-to-end data analysis using Excel, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and business recommendations that help organizations improve customer retention and reduce churn.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains customer-related information with the following columns:

- Customer ID
- Gender
- Senior Citizen
- Partner
- Dependents
- Tenure
- Phone Service
- Multiple Lines
- Internet Service
- Online Security
- Online Backup
- Device Protection
- Tech Support
- Streaming TV
- Streaming Movies
- Contract
- Paperless Billing
- Payment Method
- Monthly Charges
- Total Charges
- Churn

---

## Business Requirements

### 1. Churn Analysis

- Analyze overall customer churn.
- Identify customer segments with the highest churn rates.

### 2. Contract Analysis

- Evaluate churn across different contract types.
- Determine which contract plans have the highest customer turnover.

### 3. Tenure Analysis

- Analyze the relationship between customer tenure and churn.
- Identify periods where customers are most likely to leave.

### 4. Monthly Charges Analysis

- Examine how monthly charges influence churn.
- Identify pricing segments associated with higher churn rates.

### 5. Payment Method Analysis

- Determine churn patterns across payment methods.
- Identify payment methods linked to better customer retention.

### 6. Internet Service Analysis

- Compare churn among different internet service types.
- Identify services associated with higher customer attrition.
